FAQ

What is the entry code for?

It's how we check people in. Sign up, keep the code, show it at the meeting point.

Can I bring friends?

Yes. Everyone needs their own code — send them this page.

Do I need to own crypto?

No. If you think people shouldn't be written off for being in crypto, you're a misfit.

Is this a token or a sale?

No. Misfits is a movement. We will never ask you to buy anything to take part.

Is it peaceful?

Yes. Respect the city, the police and each other.
