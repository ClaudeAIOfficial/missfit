Who we are

Being a misfit
is the badge.

Say you work in crypto and watch the room change. You're a gambler, a grifter, a joke. Whole careers, communities and friendships get written off with one word: scam.

Misfits started as a meme and became a movement. Builders, artists, traders, devs, students and parents who are done hiding what they do. We don't ask anyone to buy anything. We ask to be treated like people.

What we stand for

Our demands

  1. 01

    End the stigma.

    Stop treating everyone in crypto as a scammer by default.

  2. 02

    Judge the person, not the wallet.

    At work, at the bank, at the dinner table — what you hold says nothing about who you are.

  3. 03

    Give us a seat at the table.

    Talk with crypto people, not just about them.

FAQ

Questions

What is the entry code for?

It's how we check people in on the day. Sign up with your email, keep the code, and show it at the meeting point.

Can I bring friends?

Yes — but every person needs their own code. Send them this page.

Do I need to own crypto to join?

No. If you believe people shouldn't be written off for being in crypto, you're a misfit.

Is this a token or a sale?

No. Misfits is a movement. We will never ask you to buy anything to take part.

Is the protest peaceful?

Yes. Misfits is a peaceful movement. Respect the city, the police and each other.
